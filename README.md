## What it does

`main.py` writes one single-line joke about office life to `/output/entry.txt`.
The joke is a fresh, specific office observation — the Wi-Fi dies and a room
full of colleagues is suddenly forced to make eye contact like it is 1994 —
instead of the tired "this meeting could have been an email" line.

## The joke

> The office Wi-Fi went down for ten minutes today and we all just sat there making awkward eye contact like it was 1994.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the joke (119 characters, one line, UTF-8) to `/output/entry.txt`.
